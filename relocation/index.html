Your State Income Tax Savings

How much do you save by moving to Florida? Florida has no state income tax, so on a $350,000 income you keep about $29,000 a year compared to California, about $21,000 compared to New York, and about $20,000 compared to New Jersey - and more once local taxes like New York City's are added on top. Over ten years the California gap alone is nearly $290,000. Pick your state, enter your income, and the calculator below runs your exact number.
