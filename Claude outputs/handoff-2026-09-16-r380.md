## 1. MORTGAGE CALCULATOR (_worker.js, CALC_MORTGAGE)

**The bug.** Taxes were `(price - 50000) * total millage` for a primary residence. Florida
homestead takes the first $25,000 off all taxes but the second $25,000 only off non-school
taxes. The calculator was exempting $25,000 too much from the school levy, so every
homestead estimate ran low (Orange, $700K home: $905 a month shown, $918 correct).

**The fix.** Each county now carries its school millage (`s`) alongside the existing
total (`m`):
- school base = price - 25,000, taxed at school mills
- other base = price - 50,000, taxed at (total - school) mills
- second home or investment = full price at total mills (unchanged)

School mills used (2025-26 adopted):
Orange 6.449 (OCPS, per West Orange Times), Seminole 5.249 (3.001 RLE + 2.248, Seminole PA
final 2025), Osceola 5.306 (Osceola PA final 2025), Polk 5.290 (Polk PA final 2025),
Volusia 5.279 (Volusia School Board), Lake 6.054 (Lake County Schools, 2026-27 proposed
figure, the closest verified number; 2025-26 was not published on their page).
The county totals were left as they were.

**The label.** New `#mc-mill-note` under the Primary Residence and Physician Loan
checkboxes, updated on every change, for example: "Orange County, about 16.70 mills in
total, 6.45 of them school taxes. Homestead takes $25,000 off the school portion and
$50,000 off everything else." Non-primary shows "No homestead exemption on a second home or
investment property."

Verified in the browser on /buying-a-home/ at 412 and 1440, $700K home: Orange $918 primary
and $974 second home, Seminole $840 and $893, Polk $932 and $992. All match hand math.
Evaluated constant has no backtick or `${`. Only line 602 of the worker changed.

Note for later - the Florida homestead amendment on the November 3, 2026 ballot would raise
the non-school exemption to $150K in 2027 and $250K in 2028. School taxes are excluded, so
the new split structure handles it with one number change if it passes. Amendment 5
(2024) indexes the second $25,000 to inflation, so it now sits slightly above $25,000. The
calculator uses $25,000, a few dollars a year conservative.
