## r374 - THE GHOST WATERMARK r371 MISSED

Commit message: `r374 - remove the class-styled ghost watermark on /about/`

**1 file: `about/index.html`.**

Sean: "Didn't I ask you to remove all ghost watermarks site-wide?" He did, in
r371, and one survived.

**Why it survived.** The r371 pass used a regex that required the opacity to sit
in the element's own inline `style` attribute:

```
<span[^>]*aria-hidden="true"[^>]*opacity:0\.0[0-9][^>]*>...</span>
```

That matched all 137 of the inline-styled ones. `/about/` carries a different
construction - a class:

```
<span class="abd-ghost" aria-hidden="true">A</span>
.abd-ghost{position:absolute;top:12px;right:-8px;font-family:'Cormorant Garamond';
           font-size:clamp(170px,28vw,320px);color:#0D1B2E;opacity:.045;...}
```

A 320px ghosted "A". No opacity in the markup, so the regex could not see it.
**Grep is not reading, and it cannot see a class at all.**

**Fix.** The span is removed. The `.abd-ghost` rule is left in place and inert,
matching what r371 did with the other watermark rules. Balance unchanged.

**New permanent gate: `scripts/ghostcheck.mjs`.** It renders each page and
reports any element carrying short text at computed opacity <= 0.2 in a font >=
40px, plus any `::before` / `::after` matching the same test - so it catches a
ghost however it is built: inline style, class, or pseudo-element. Run it on all
132 pages before claiming a sitewide removal is complete.

It has one requirement: scroll the page and wait about 2s before measuring.
`.section-heading` reveals on an IntersectionObserver, and a heading caught
mid-transition reads as a faint 45px element. Without the scroll-and-settle the
first run reported seven false positives on real headings.

**Result: 132 pages scanned, 1 ghost found, 1 removed, 0 remaining.**

**Verified:** 132 pages return 200, `node --check` passes, 6 computed-style

## r373 - THE HERO, FIXED AT THE SOURCE

Commit message: `r373 - hero headroom baked into the art, full bleed`

**60 files: 9 state pages, 24 -hd images, 27 -mob images.**

Sean, on the live r372 pages: "Where did the brown translucent border around the
hero image come from? Why is it there?" It was the r372 blurred backdrop. r371
manufactured headroom with a solid strip (read as a black box), r372 filled the
strip and the letterbox space with a blurred copy of the art (read as a brown
frame). Both were the same mistake: trying to make headroom outside the image.

**Root cause, measured on a 10% grid over each source.** Florida's top edge sits
at about 4% of the file height and the home state's at about 15%, so a 679px-tall
hero has roughly 27px of sky above the artwork. The fixed header is 89-97px. The
art simply has no headroom, so nothing done in CSS can give it any without
inventing pixels.

**Fix: put the headroom in the image.** `r373_headroom.py` grows the top 20 rows
of each file (pure sky on all eight; Florida starts around row 27, so a deeper
mirror duplicated the panhandle above itself and had to be thrown out) into a
110px band, stretched, blurred on a ramp so the seam stays continuous, and
darkened toward the top. Output `<name>-hd.*` at 1600x789, aspect 2.028.
New York is untouched at 1600x893: its artwork already starts 20% down, which is
172px of headroom at desktop width, and it clears the nav on its own.

The page CSS is now the simplest possible hero, with no ::before, no padding and
no bars:

```
#sb-stx-hero{height:calc(100vw / <aspect>);max-height:100svh;overflow:hidden;}
#sb-stx-hero img{position:absolute;inset:0;width:100%;height:100%;
                 object-fit:cover;object-position:<pos>;}
```

Height clamps to the viewport (panel above the fold) and to the file's own aspect
(the art is only ever cropped at the bottom, never at the sides - the state
outlines run almost to both edges, so a side crop is the one crop these images
cannot take). object-position is `center top` on the eight, `center 35%` on New
York so its crop comes off both ends.

**Phones get their own file.** At 412px the desktop composition is a 203px band,
and the 97px header plus the 120px panel cover most of it. `r373_mobile.py`
builds `<name>-mob.*` at 1200x1159 (New York 1200x1320): the same art at full
width, 300px of generated sky above it and 350px of foreground below it. The
lower band is blurred hard and taken to near black on a power-0.32 ramp, because
a lightly blurred stretch of grass, water and boats reads as a smear while a fast
fade reads as a vignette. `<picture>` serves it under
`media="(max-width:767px)"` and the CSS switches aspect at the same breakpoint.
Result at 412px: 103px of sky for the header, 175px of art with nothing on top of
it, 120px of vignette for the panel.

`og:image`, `twitter:image` and the JSON-LD absolute URLs were repointed to the
`-hd` files, and the `<img>` intrinsic size attributes were corrected - they said
1600x893 on files that are 679 tall.

## TRAPS LEARNED (cumulative, new ones at the end)

9. Check every property the design system specifies, not just colour.
10. "Removed as dead" needs proof. Diff the computed result before deleting an
    override.
11. Audit the whole site, not the pages you edited.
12. A component's own source file is not proof of what renders.
13. jsdom stubs must go in `beforeParse`.
14. The handoff can be stale about the build of record. List Drive first.
15. Deliver into the repo clone, not as a ZIP.
16. Outbound delivery caps at 30 MiB; inbound uploads do not. Never tell Sean his
    upload failed.
17. The site carries 5.74 MiB of unreferenced assets. Do not delete blind.
18. `<\/script>` in raw HTML does not close a script element. Emit
    `'</scr' + 'ipt>'`.
19. A stale dev server will make a working fix look unapplied.
20. Check before retrying after a relay error. The tool says the reply may have
    been lost, not the write. Verify by size and mtime first.
21. Animated numbers need a floor.
22. When Sean says a number looks wrong, measure it.
23. **Measure the image and the box before touching a crop.** The hero's width
    and height attributes said 1600x893; `naturalWidth` said 1600x679. The attrs
    were wrong and reasoning from them would have produced the wrong fix.
24. **A fixed header over a hero is part of the crop.** The desktop hero cropped
    almost nothing and the states were still cut off, because the nav covered
    them. Check what overlays the element, not only the element.
25. **fullPage screenshots lie about scroll-revealed text and masked images.**
    Scroll before capturing, use element screenshots for masks, and measure
    computed values rather than trusting the picture.
26. **Prove a ported component with a computed-style diff against its source
    page**, not by eye. Cheap, exact, and it catches a missing style block.
27. **Run contrast differentially, not absolutely.** An absolute pass flags
    white-on-photo hero text as a failure. What matters is whether the new build
    added a finding the old one did not have.
28. **The header fades itself out at any scroll position.** `sb-fade` in
    `SB_NAV_MORPH` drops it to opacity 0 after 1.5s of no scrolling, including at
    the top of the page. Anything sized or coloured to sit behind the nav has to
    look right with the nav gone.
29. **Never leave a flat rectangle where art should be.** A blurred, scaled copy
    of the page's own hero image as a `::before` backdrop fills the header strip
    and any letterbox space with something that reads as part of the design, and
    costs no new assets.
30. **Fit a hero with `height:min(viewport, natural)` plus `object-fit:contain`,
    not with a crop.** It guarantees the overlay text is above the fold at every
    window size and never cuts the subject.
31. **Check per-file dimensions before writing one aspect ratio into a shared
    template.** Eight of the nine relocation heroes are 2.356; New York is 1.792.
32. **When an image has no headroom, fix the image.** Two revisions were spent
    manufacturing space around the art in CSS - a solid strip, then a blurred
    backdrop - and both read as a frame Sean rejected on sight. Growing the sky
    inside the file took one script and left the page markup simpler than it
    started.
33. **Measure where the subject sits before extending a canvas.** Mirroring a
    110px band upward duplicated the top of Florida above itself, because Florida
    starts at row 27. Only the first 20 rows are safe to grow from.
34. **A linear fade is not enough to hide a stretched foreground.** Grass, water
    and boats stayed recognisable as vertical streaks for the first hundred
    pixels. A power-0.32 ramp to near black reads as a vignette instead.
35. **A post-check that reuses the removal's own pattern proves nothing.** r371
