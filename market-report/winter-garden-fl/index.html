What is the median home price in Winter Garden, FL?

The August 2026 median sale price for a single-family home in Winter Garden was $670,066. Among the ten Central Florida markets we track, Winter Garden ranks number 4 by median sale price, roughly 19% below Lake Nona at $832,250 and about 3% above Lake Mary at $650,000.

A single month of closings is a small sample in most of these markets, so we lead with a three month average rather than a month-over-month change. Across June, July and August the Winter Garden median averaged $698,022, up 2.05% against the same three months a year earlier. That window smooths the mix of what happened to close and matches the season, which a comparison against last month cannot do.

Median price per square foot was $255. Taken against the median sale price, that implies a typical Winter Garden transaction of roughly 2,628 square feet. That is a derived figure rather than a reported one, and it is useful mainly as a sanity check on what the inventory in the City of Winter Garden actually consists of.

One caution on reading a single month. Medians move with the mix of what closed, not only with underlying value. A month in which a handful of higher-priced or lower-priced homes close can shift the median without anything changing for a specific property. Treat the direction across three months as the signal and any single month as noise.

Is Winter Garden a buyer's or seller's market?

Winter Garden is currently classified as a balanced market, on 3.70 months of supply. We classify on months of supply: under three months is a seller's market, three to six is balanced, and over six favors buyers. Those thresholds are ours, not the data source's, and we state them so you can judge the label rather than take it on faith.

Across the ten markets we track: 3 seller's markets, 7 balanced markets. Winter Garden sits at 3.70 months, near the middle of the ten.

With 479 active listings against 3.70 months of supply, Winter Garden is absorbing on the order of 129 sales per month. For a seller, that absorption number often matters more than the median, because it describes how much company a listing has rather than what the market did last month.

A market with smaller, older homes on smaller lots can carry a higher per-square-foot figure than a market with a much higher median, simply because the homes are smaller. How long homes are taking to sell

Median days on market for Winter Garden was 22 in August 2026, against a ten-market median near 21.5. Sellers received a median of 98.5% of their last list price.

This is median cumulative days on market straight from Stellar MLS, so it counts the days a listing sat before going under contract rather than an estimate. Read it next to percent of last list price. A market that sells quickly and close to ask is tight; one that sells quickly only after reductions is not, and the two numbers together tell you which you are looking at.

What this means if you are selling

With 479 active listings and 3.70 months of supply, the pricing decision carries real weight. At roughly 129 absorptions a month against that inventory, a Winter Garden listing is not scarce simply by virtue of its address.

The practical consequence is that the first two weeks of exposure do most of the work. An aspirationally priced listing rarely gets corrected by a competing offer; it accumulates days and invites a reduction. What this means if you are buying

Supply sits near the middle of the region, which makes timing matter more than leverage. 479 active listings and 22 median days on market describe the market as a whole, not any particular street.

Submarkets inside Winter Garden behave differently from the aggregate. Waterfront, gated, and golf-adjacent inventory can stay tight while a broader median softens, and the reverse happens too.
