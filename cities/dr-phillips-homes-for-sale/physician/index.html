The Tax Advantage

Florida levies no state income tax - a meaningful, recurring factor for physicians relocating from higher-tax states.

Relocation Logistics

Many lenders offer physician mortgage products - low or no down payment, no PMI, and favorable treatment of student debt - for homes in Dr. Phillips's price range. Florida medical licensure and hospital credentialing typically take a few months, so it's worth planning for that transition window when timing a purchase.
