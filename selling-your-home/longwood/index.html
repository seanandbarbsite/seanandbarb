What Longwood Sellers Ask

What drives value in the Markham Woods / 32779 corridor?

Lot size, gating, and golf access more than finished square footage. An acre-plus lot behind a guarded gate in Alaqua or the Hills of Alaqua trades on a different basis than a smaller home on a standard lot, even at similar interior size. The 32779 school reputation - among Seminole County's strongest - adds a premium baked into nearly every sale.

Which Longwood communities hold value best?

The guard-gated golf and estate communities - Alaqua Lakes and the Hills of Alaqua - lead, followed by the larger-lot stretches of the Markham Woods Corridor and Wingfield North. Bear Lake adds lakefront appeal. Limited supply of true estate product keeps demand firm across the corridor.

How fast are Longwood luxury homes selling?

Longwood's 32779 has been one of the faster-absorbing luxury markets in the region, with strong demand and appreciation. Correctly priced estate homes move quickly; the risk is overpricing in a market where buyers have clear comparables and act decisively.

How important is presentation for an estate home in Longwood?

Essential - large lots and acreage are sold as much on lifestyle as on the house. Aerial photography, twilight imagery, and marketing that conveys privacy, land, and the gate experience are what move estate buyers. A home that photographs as just another big house leaves money on the table.

How does Sean & Barb market a Longwood estate?

Through Sotheby's international reach plus targeted campaigns to relocation and move-up buyers prioritizing schools, acreage, and gated privacy. Sean & Barb also tap direct relationships with executives and physicians relocating into the corporate corridor - buyers who want exactly what 32779 offers and will pay for it.
