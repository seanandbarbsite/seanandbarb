Selling in Orlando Requires Knowing Orlando.

Orlando is not one market - it's a dozen. The historic luxury core (College Park, Thornton Park, Delaney Park) trades on lake frontage, brick streets, and walkability, while the citywide median is pulled down by far larger volumes of entry-level and investor product. For a luxury seller, the headline number is the wrong number - what matters is the comparable set three blocks in either direction.

Orlando's citywide median sits near the Central Florida metro average, but the urban-core luxury segment runs well above it. Pricing a College Park or Baldwin Park home off a general Orlando median is the most common - and most expensive - mistake a seller can make.

Why is the citywide Orlando median misleading for a luxury seller?

Which Orlando neighborhoods hold value best at the high end?The historic urban core has shown the most pricing resilience: College Park along Lake Adair and Lake Ivanhoe, Thornton Park around Lake Eola, Delaney Park, and Lake Eola Heights. Limited supply, walkability, and architectural character that newer construction cannot replicate all support value. Baldwin Park's New Urbanist design holds a distinct, loyal buyer pool of its own.
How long are well-priced luxury homes taking to sell in Orlando?Correctly priced and well-presented urban-core homes in the $700K-$1.5M range generally move within the first several weeks. Above $2M the buyer pool narrows and marketing reach matters more - out-of-state and relocation buyers become a larger share, and the right exposure can be the difference between 30 days and six months. Sean & Barb have closed throughout the range.
How important is presentation and photography in Orlando?Critical. Urban-core buyers are visually sophisticated and shop online first. In a market where a citywide search returns hundreds of listings, professional photography, staging, and a strong first frame determine whether your home gets showings in week one or sits unseen. Presentation is where the luxury core separates itself from the broader Orlando inventory.
