Chapter Two

Why is there never a lockbox on our listings?

No lockbox has ever hung on a Sean and Barb listing. No Supra Key on the door letting strangers walk your home unsupervised. We personally attend every showing, and the reasons are practical: your privacy and belongings stay protected, your home is presented by someone who knows its story, buyer reactions reach us firsthand instead of thirdhand, and access to your home stays on your terms. It is slower for us and better for you, which is the point of luxury representation.

Chapter Three

Why do physicians relocating to Orlando choose us?

Because relocation is a deadline problem before it is a house problem. We plan backward from your start date at AdventHealth, Orlando Health, Nemours, or the Orlando VA so physician mortgage approval, licensing, closing, and moving arrive together instead of colliding.

Florida has no state or local income tax. A physician earning $500K keeps roughly $23K a year versus New Jersey, $24K versus New York, and $34K versus California. Run your own numbers with the calculator in our physician relocation guide.
