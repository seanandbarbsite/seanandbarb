How Much Will You Save on Florida Income Tax?

How much do you keep by moving to Florida? On a $500,000 income, someone relocating from California keeps about $45,000 more each year, since Florida has no state or local income tax. From New Jersey the figure is about $30,000 a year; from New York City, about $51,000 once the city income tax is included. Over ten years that is roughly $450,000 to $510,000 kept.
