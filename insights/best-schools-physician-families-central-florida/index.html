Best Schools for Physician Families in Central Florida: A-Rated Public and Top Private

Both major school districts serving Central Florida's luxury markets hold A state grades, and the region adds nationally ranked private schools led by Windermere Preparatory. For relocating physician families, strong schools are often the deciding factor in choosing a neighborhood, and Central Florida delivers across both public and private options.

A-rated public districts

District State grade Notes
Orange County Public Schools (OCPS) A, second consecutive year 96 traditional schools earned an A in 2024-25
Seminole County Public Schools (SCPS) A, consistently #1 district in Central Florida; 2nd among Florida's 14 largest districts

Which district you are in depends on where you live. OCPS serves Orlando, Winter Garden, Windermere, Dr. Phillips, Lake Nona, Winter Park, and Maitland. SCPS serves Longwood and Oviedo. Families who prioritize Seminole County's consistent top ranking often focus their home search on Longwood and Oviedo.

Leading private schools

Central Florida's flagship private school is Windermere Preparatory School (6189 Winter Garden-Vineland Road, Windermere, FL 34786), an IB World School affiliated with Nord Anglia Education and ranked the number one private K-12 school in Orlando by Niche. It is a natural fit for families settling in Windermere.

Other strong options include Lake Highland Preparatory and Trinity Preparatory. Tuition at the top private schools generally runs in the high-$20,000s to mid-$30,000s per year for upper grades; confirm current figures directly with each school, as tuition is updated annually.

How schools shape the home search

Because school assignment follows address, your school priorities should guide your neighborhood choice from the start.
