Pricing a Luxury Home in a Balanced Market: Why the First Two Weeks Decide Everything

In today's Central Florida market, the price you set in the first two weeks largely determines your outcome. With homes taking around 32 days to sell and price reductions appearing on roughly 22% of active listings, an overpriced luxury home sits, ages, and often sells for less than a sharply priced one would have. The peak-market habit of "pricing high and leaving room" no longer works.

What changed

The market has rebalanced. Inventory is higher than a year ago, buyers have more time, and price reductions are far more common than during the bidding-war years, per recent reporting from sources including the Orlando Regional REALTOR Association and Redfin. Buyers now compare your home against more options and they notice when something is priced ahead of the comps.

How luxury sellers should price now

Three principles matter more than ever:

Price to current comps, not last year's peak. Equity gains from recent years are real, but the buyer is paying today's market. A price anchored to verifiable recent sales attracts showings in the critical first two weeks, when buyer attention is highest.

Invest in presentation. In a market with choices, the best-presented home wins. Professional staging, photography, and pre-list repairs return more than they cost when buyers have alternatives.

Use concessions strategically. A targeted closing-cost contribution can move a specific motivated buyer to close while preserving your headline price, often more effective than an across-the-board price cut.

The bottom line

A well-priced, well-presented luxury home still sells well in Central Florida. The mistake is treating today's market like the peak. Price it right from day one and you control the outcome; price it high and the market will correct you slowly and expensively.

Frequently asked questions

How important is pricing in the current Central Florida market? Decisive. With longer days on market and frequent price reductions, pricing to current comps from day one is the single biggest lever on your result.

Should I offer concessions when selling? Often yes; a targeted closing-cost contribution can help a motivated buyer close while preserving your headline price.
