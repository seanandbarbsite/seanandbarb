Establishing Florida Domicile Correctly: A Physician's Guide to Avoiding Residency Audits

Buying a Florida home does not, by itself, make you a Florida resident for tax purposes. Florida's zero income tax only protects you once you have genuinely established legal domicile, and high-tax states defend their revenue with aggressive residency audits. For a physician saving tens of thousands a year, getting domicile right is what makes the savings real and audit-proof.

Why this matters more for physicians

New York and California are especially aggressive about residency audits. Physically moving is not enough if you keep a home, employment, or close ties in your prior state. New York's "convenience of the employer" rule is particularly complex for physicians who continue to treat New York patients remotely, so if any part of your practice stays connected to a high-tax state, plan carefully and document everything.

The essential steps to airtight domicile

No single action establishes domicile. Auditors look at the total picture, so the goal is a consistent body of evidence that the center of your life is now Florida:

  • File a Declaration of Domicile with your Florida county clerk, the sworn statement that Florida is your permanent home
  • Get a Florida driver's license and surrender your prior state license
  • Register your vehicles in Florida
  • Register to vote in Florida and actually vote
  • Spend the majority of the year in Florida, and document your days; many states use a 183-day threshold
  • Update your address everywhere: banks, brokerages, insurers, employer payroll, subscriptions, and the IRS
  • Move your financial life: establish Florida banking and primary financial relationships
  • Update estate planning documents (will, trusts, powers of attorney) to Florida
  • Claim the Florida homestead exemption on your primary residence, which is both a tax benefit and strong domicile evidence
  • Shift family and personal ties: enroll children in local schools, move valued possessions and pets, join local organizations
  • Sever or reduce ties to the prior state, and file a final part-year resident return there
  • Keep records: travel logs, utility bills, and receipts that corroborate your Florida presence

The homestead exemption in particular does double duty. It lowers your property taxes and signals to auditors that this is genuinely your primary home. We cover it fully in Florida Homestead Exemption and Save Our Homes.

The payoff

Done correctly, domicile locks in the savings detailed in How Much Will Physicians Save Moving to Florida, which can reach roughly $104,600 per year for a $1 million earner relocating from New York. The paperwork is modest next to the money it protects.

Frequently asked questions

Does buying a Florida home make me a Florida resident for taxes? Not on its own. Residency depends on legal domicile, which means shifting and documenting the center of your life.

What is a Declaration of Domicile? A sworn statement filed with your county clerk declaring Florida as your permanent home. Helpful evidence, but not sufficient alone.

How many days do I need to spend in Florida? Many states use 183 days as a guideline, but auditors weigh where your life is centered, not just days counted.
