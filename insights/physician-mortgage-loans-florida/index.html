Physician Mortgage Loans in Florida: 0% Down, No PMI, and Student Loans Excluded

For a physician buying in Central Florida's $1M-plus luxury market, a physician mortgage loan is almost always the smarter financing choice. These programs are built for your financial profile: low or zero down payment, no PMI, student loans left out of your debt ratio, and the ability to close before your first day of work. Here is how they stack up and who offers them locally.

Physician loan vs conventional jumbo

Feature Physician loan Conventional jumbo
Down payment 0% to 5% on $1M+ homes 10% to 20% typically required
PMI None, ever Required under 20% down
Student loan treatment Excluded from DTI Counted fully against you
Pre-start-date closing Yes, with employment contract No
Loan limits Up to $2.5M (some lenders) Conforming limit ~$832,751

The student-loan treatment is often the deciding factor. Many physicians carry six figures of student debt that would sink a conventional debt-to-income calculation. Physician loans exclude it, which is what makes a $1M-plus purchase approvable early in a career.

The Central Florida buying-power advantage

In a market where $1M to $3M homes are the physician target zone, the down-payment difference is dramatic. A $1.5M purchase with a physician loan requires roughly $75,000 to $150,000 down, versus about $300,000 on a conventional jumbo. That is $150,000 or more retained, capital that can keep compounding instead of sitting in home equity. You can model your own numbers with our physician mortgage calculator.

Frequently asked questions

What is a physician mortgage loan? A specialized loan with low or zero down, no PMI, student loans excluded from DTI, and pre-start-date closing with an employment contract.

Can a physician buy a $1.5M home with little money down? Yes. Expect roughly $75,000 to $150,000 down versus about $300,000 conventional, keeping $150,000 or more invested.

Do physician loans charge PMI? No, never, even with low down payments.
