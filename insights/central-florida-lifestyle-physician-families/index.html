The Physician Family's Lifestyle Guide to Central Florida: Spas, MICHELIN Dining, and More

Beyond top hospitals and A-rated schools, Central Florida has quietly become one of the Southeast's best places to live well, with a MICHELIN-recognized dining scene, world-class spas, and luxury shopping. When you and your family are off the clock, this is what the region offers.

A MICHELIN-recognized dining scene

Orlando was added to the MICHELIN Guide and has rapidly become one of America's most exciting culinary cities. In 2025, 59 Orlando restaurants were recognized, including 9 starred venues, 15 Bib Gourmands, and 35 Recommended restaurants.

World-class spas

For the physician household that needs to decompress, the region's spa scene is genuinely top-tier:

  • The Ritz-Carlton Spa, Orlando (Grande Lakes), nature-inspired treatments at the Ritz-Carlton standard, arguably the finest in Central Florida
  • Allora Day Spa, an award-winning day spa with locations including Dr. Phillips / Sand Lake and Winter Park
  • SpaWell, Lake Nona, convenient for the Medical City community
  • Woodhouse Spa, Altamonte Springs, a polished option for the north suburbs

Luxury shopping, salons, and culture

Central Florida pairs its outdoor, lake-and-golf lifestyle with upscale shopping, top hair salons, and a growing arts and gallery scene. From the boutiques and design showrooms around Winter Park and Dr. Phillips to the cultural venues downtown, there is plenty to fill a weekend off call.
