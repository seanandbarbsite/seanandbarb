Where Physicians Live: Central Florida's Luxury Communities, From Windermere to Lake Nona

Physicians relocating to Central Florida tend to concentrate in a handful of luxury communities chosen for proximity to the major hospitals, top-rated schools, and the region's lakes and golf. The right neighborhood depends on where you practice and your call schedule. This is an overview; each city page goes deeper with listings, school details, and exact hospital drive times.

Windermere: the crown jewel

Windermere is a small town of under 3,000 residents wrapped around the Butler Chain of Lakes, one of the most pristine navigable lake chains in Florida. It is the pinnacle of Central Florida luxury.

Dr. Phillips: Orlando's premier medical address

Dr. Phillips (zip 32819) is a favorite for physicians who want luxury minutes from the AdventHealth and Orlando Health corridors. Its established neighborhoods include Bay Hill, Sand Lake Hills, Phillips Landing, Orange Tree, Vizcaya, Turtle Creek, and Butler Bay. See the Dr. Phillips page.

Lake Nona: precision living by the Medical City

For physicians practicing in the Lake Nona Medical City ecosystem, living minutes from work in a master-planned, innovation-focused community is hard to beat. See the Lake Nona page.

Winter Park: old Florida elegance on the Chain of Lakes

Winter Park offers historic charm, tree-lined brick streets, and lakefront estates, with quick access to Orlando's hospitals and culture. See the Winter Park page.

Frequently asked questions

Where do most physicians live in Central Florida? Primarily Windermere, Dr. Phillips, Lake Nona, and Winter Park, chosen for hospitals, schools, and luxury living.

What is the most exclusive community near Orlando? Windermere, with Isleworth, Keene's Pointe, and Golden Oak around the Butler Chain of Lakes.

What price range should a relocating physician expect? Generally $1M to $3M, with ultra-luxury estates ranging well above that.
