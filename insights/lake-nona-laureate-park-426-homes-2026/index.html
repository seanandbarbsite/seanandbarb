426 New Homes Coming to Lake Nona: What It Means for Relocating Physicians

Tavistock has won approval to add 426 new homesites to Lake Nona's Laureate Park, just south of Medical City, which means more fresh inventory is coming to one of the most physician-friendly addresses in Central Florida. For doctors planning a move to the Lake Nona hospital and research cluster, that is welcome news in a market where well-located luxury homes near work can be hard to find.

What was approved

According to GrowthSpotter, Orlando's Development Review Committee approved an expansion of Laureate Park that will bring 426 single-family homesites west of Luminary Boulevard and south of Medical City, built in two phases, with neighborhood parks and related infrastructure. You can read the original reporting on GrowthSpotter.

Why it matters if you practice in Lake Nona

Lake Nona's Medical City is one of the biggest reasons physicians choose this part of Central Florida, anchored by the UCF College of Medicine ecosystem and a growing cluster of hospitals and research institutions. New construction this close to that hub is meaningful for two reasons:

First, it adds inventory in an area where demand from medical professionals consistently outpaces supply. More homesites means more chances to buy near work rather than commuting in from farther out.

Second, new-construction phases tend to release in waves. Physicians who plan ahead, especially those timing a purchase around a start date, can position themselves for an early-phase homesite instead of competing for limited resale stock. The bigger picture

This is one more signal that Central Florida's medical corridor keeps expanding, alongside major hospital investments across the region.
